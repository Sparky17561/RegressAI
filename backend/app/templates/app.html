How to read these results

  • Verdict (Improved / Regression) is a comparison between NEW and OLD.
    “Regression” means NEW introduces higher risk than OLD — even if parts of it look better.
  • Cookedness is an absolute risk score.
    Low cookedness = generally safe output. High cookedness = misleading, unsafe, or legally risky output.
  • Quality and Safety are judged separately.
    NEW can be higher quality but still marked as Regression if safety worsens.
  • Rule of thumb:
    Verdict answers “Is NEW better than OLD?” Cookedness answers “How dangerous is this output?”
